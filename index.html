About

Xing Lan is an Associate Professor and Ph.D. supervisor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Northeastern University, China. He received his Ph.D. in Engineering from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in June 2025 and was a visiting scholar at the National University of Singapore.

His research interests include multimodal large models, image and video understanding, and embodied intelligence. He has published more than 20 papers in leading journals and conferences, including over 10 papers in CCF-A venues and IEEE Transactions, and holds two granted Chinese invention patents.

He has received the President's Award of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, the CAS ZhuLi Yuehua Award, the 3DMM Best Student Paper Award, and the ChinaMM Best Poster Award. He is a member of the Cloud-Network Intelligent Computing Group.

Students

I am looking for motivated M.S. and Ph.D. students for the 2026 and 2027 admissions cycles. Prospective students interested in multimodal large models, image and video understanding, and embodied intelligence are welcome to get in touch.

  • Openings: 4-6 M.S. students and 1-2 Ph.D. students per year.
  • Training: Students are encouraged to gain hands-on experience through research projects and industry internships after meeting school requirements.

  • Expectations: Applicants from diverse backgrounds are welcome. Self-motivation and strong implementation skills are valued.
